Figure 15 In CWM-RL, model weights can be updated at any time on the worker side: between trajectories, within a 
trajectory between steps, or even during token generation. Compared to traditional RL, this removes all synchronization 
overhead, maximizing worker throughput while minimizing idle time. In exchange for never blocking inference, we 
accept that trajectories will potentially use mixed weights, though frequent model updates ensure that generations 
remain reasonably on-policy.
