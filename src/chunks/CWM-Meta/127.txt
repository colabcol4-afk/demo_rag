supports batched inference, CUDA graphs, paged attention (Kwon et al., 2023), chunked prefills, host-side KV- 
cache, tensor parallelism, and CPU/GPU profiling. In batched inference, one generates tokens for each sequence 
in a batch in parallel, continuing without synchronizing CUDA streams until a block of tokens (e.g., 32) is 
completed. After each block, completed sequences are truncated at stop tokens and returned, and new sequences 
are added to the batch so as to keep the batch size constant. For more details, see Carbonneaux (2025).
